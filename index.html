About me

Hi, I'm Bruno. I live in Zagreb and I'm an experienced software engineer with a real passion for building things with computers.

I've worked as a full-stack software engineer for the last 5+ years, on everything from telecom applications to a blockchain fantasy football game. Starting with an empty file and ending up with something people can actually use has never stopped being fun.

Besides that, I love listening to indie music, reading a bunch of books, watching movies and doing architecture and film photography.

How I work

Most of my backend work is in Java, usually Spring Boot (sometimes Quarkus). I have experience with both large enterprise systems and startups, and with monoliths and microservices. I care a lot about writing something another person can still read and work with months or years later.

On the frontend it's mostly TypeScript and React, with enough Angular and Vue behind me to know the framework matters far less than the person using it.
