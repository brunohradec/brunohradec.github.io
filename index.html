About me

Hi, I'm Bruno. I live in Zagreb and I'm into creative things of all kinds: movies, books, photography and above all making stuff with computers.

I've worked as a full-stack software engineer for the last 5+ years, on everything from telecom applications to a blockchain fantasy football game. Starting with an empty file and ending up with something people can actually use has never stopped being fun.

How I work

Most of my backend work is Java, and usually Spring Boot (sometimes Quarkus). A lot of it has been data-heavy, the kind where the query plan matters and people notice when it doesn't. I care a lot about writing something another person can still read and work with months or years later.

On the frontend it's TypeScript and React, with enough Angular and Vue behind me to know the framework matters far less than the people using the thing. I do design work and consulting on UX/UI stuff too as a part of my frontend work.
